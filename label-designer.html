PREMIUM SHOE CARE
Bring every step back to life
250 mL
DIRECTIONS
1. Remove loose dirt and dust from the shoes.

2. Apply a small amount of foam to the surface using a suitable shoe brush or microfiber towel.

3. Gently clean in a circular motion.

4. Wipe away residue with a clean, dry microfiber towel.

5. Allow the shoes to air dry completely before use.
WARNING
For external use only. Do not ingest. Avoid contact with eyes. Keep out of reach of children. Test on a small, inconspicuous area before full application. Do not use on damaged or severely wet surfaces.
STORAGE
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive heat. Keep container tightly closed when not in use.
INGREDIENTS
Aqua, Coco-Glucoside, Glycerin, PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil, Phenoxyethanol, Parfum, Jojoba Oil, Tetrasodium EDTA.
